Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TTT Tic Tac Toe ("the App") handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

TTT Tic Tac Toe does not directly collect or store personal information such as your name, phone number, address, or password.

The App may store game scores, player names, and settings locally on your device to provide the game functionality.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and use certain information, such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, and information related to advertising and app usage, according to Google's policies.

For more information about how Google handles information, please visit Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

Third-party services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the App, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
